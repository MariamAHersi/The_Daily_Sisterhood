About the Publication

Ideas, examined with curiosity and nuance.

The Daily Sisterhood is an independent online publication founded by sisters with a shared passion for thoughtful writing, meaningful discussion, and honest criticism.

Inspired by the timeless style of traditional newspapers and literary journals, we publish original articles on current affairs, politics, social issues, culture, books, films, and television. Our aim is to create a space where ideas are explored with curiosity, nuance, and respect — a page that rewards a reader's attention rather than competing for their attention.

Rather than chasing headlines, we focus on thoughtful analysis, well-researched opinion pieces, and in-depth reviews that encourage readers to think critically and engage with the world around them. We would rather publish one considered essay a week than ten reactions to the day's news cycle.

Our Mission

We believe good writing should inform, challenge, and inspire. Whether examining political developments, reviewing a novel, or analysing a film, our goal is to produce thoughtful journalism that values evidence, context, and meaningful conversation over sensationalism.

Founded by Sisters

The Daily Sisterhood began as a shared notebook between siblings who disagreed often, read constantly, and wanted a place to argue in public without shouting.

The publication is currently maintained by the founding editors. As the project grows, we hope to invite guest writers and contributors who share our editorial line: curiosity first, certainty earned.

What We Value

The editorial line
  • I.

    Evidence over instinct

    Opinions are welcome. Opinions without research are not.

  • II.

    Context over speed

    We would rather be the second publication to cover a story well than the first to cover it thinly.

  • III.

    Respect over ridicule

    We can disagree sharply with an idea without treating the person who holds it with contempt.

  • IV.

    Craft over volume

    Every piece is edited for argument, evidence, and sentence — not just published to fill a schedule.
