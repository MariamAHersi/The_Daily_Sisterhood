# The Daily Sisterhood
*A digital publication exploring politics, society, culture, literature, and film.*

## About

The Daily Sisterhood is an independent online publication founded by sisters with a shared passion for thoughtful writing, meaningful discussion, and honest criticism.

Inspired by the timeless style of traditional newspapers and literary journals, The Lantern publishes original articles on current affairs, politics, social issues, culture, books, films, and television. Our aim is to create a space where ideas are explored with curiosity, nuance, and respect.

Rather than chasing headlines, we focus on thoughtful analysis, well-researched opinion pieces, and in-depth reviews that encourage readers to think critically and engage with the world around them.
 
## Features

- 📰 Original articles on politics and current affairs
- 🌍 Commentary on social and cultural issues
- 📚 Book reviews and literary essays
- 🎬 Film and television reviews
- ✍️ Opinion pieces and long-form essays
- 📖 Editorial-style design inspired by traditional newspapers

## Our Mission

We believe good writing should inform, challenge, and inspire.

Whether examining political developments, reviewing a novel, or analysing a film, our goal is to produce thoughtful journalism that values evidence, context, and meaningful conversation over sensationalism.
